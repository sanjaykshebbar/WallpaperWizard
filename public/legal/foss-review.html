Review date: 5 July 2026 · Scope: full npm dependency tree

Executive summary

Overall risk: LOW — 100% permissive licenses, no copyleft, suitable for any kind of hosting or redistribution.

AdBoard is released under the MIT License (© 2026 Sanjay KS). Its entire installed dependency tree — 378 packages (direct + transitive), of which 190 are production/shipped — resolves to permissive, business-friendly licenses. No GPL, LGPL, or AGPL license is present anywhere in the tree. The only weak-copyleft components (MPL-2.0) are build-time tools that are not redistributed with the application.

Notable licenses & what they require

  • Apache-2.0 (firebase, ~70 transitive): permissive with an express patent grant. Retain the license text and reproduce NOTICE files when redistributing.
  • MPL-2.0 (lightningcss): file-level weak copyleft, pulled in by the Tailwind/Vite build toolchain and not part of the shipped bundle — no obligations are triggered by normal distribution.
  • CC-BY-4.0 (caniuse-lite): attribution for a build-time data file; not code.
  • ISC / BSD / 0BSD / Blue Oak: MIT-equivalent in practice — keep notices.
Compliance obligations: keep the MIT notice with copies, ship the third-party notices file, preserve Apache NOTICE contents. No source-code disclosure is required by any license in the tree.
